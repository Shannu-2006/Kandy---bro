# KARTHIK – A DIVINE BIRTHDAY EXPERIENCE 🔱

A cinematic, interactive birthday website dedicated to **Karthik**, devotee of Lord Shiva.

---

## 🌟 How to View the Website

1. Go to the **`Kandy`** folder on your **Desktop**.
2. Double-click **`index.html`**.
3. It will open directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or your default browser.

---

## 🔱 Feature Highlights

### 1. Page 1 – Mahadev's Divine Trishul (6-Stage Cinematic Landing)
- **New Sacred Visual**: Lord Shiva's blue-toned hand holding the ornate metallic Trishul with realistic water splashes and Rudraksha beads (moving together as one single divine object).
- **Stage 1 (0–1s)**: Dark divine void atmosphere, subtle golden-blue celestial light, tiny floating stardust.
- **Stage 2 (1–2.5s)**: Vertical descent from above the viewport towards center with energy trail and subtle motion blur.
- **Stage 3 (2.5–3s)**: Powerful landing impact at 2.5s: screen shake, mobile vibration, controlled golden-blue flash, and expanding circular shockwaves.
- **Stage 4 (3–4s)**: Glistening water droplets spread outward with realistic gravity and a celestial water wave.
- **Stage 5 (4–5s)**: Trishul settles upright and remains completely stationary (no continuous floating or zoom). Soft divine golden-blue aura glows.
- **Stage 6 (5–6s)**: Reveal text box: *"Press the Trishul to witness the divine presence of Mahadev..."*.
- **Clickable After Landing**: Clicking the Trishul or message box triggers a golden-blue glow pulse and navigates smoothly to Page 2.

### 2. Page 2 – Mahadev's Appearance & Birthday Blessings
- **Active Video**: Plays `assets/media/mahadev_edited.mp4` on Mount Kailash.
- **Zero Zooming**: Camera remains completely stationary (`transform: none`).
- **Aspect Ratio Preserved**: Configured with `object-fit: contain` so Mahadev is never cropped.
- **Graceful Loading Screen**: Auspicious golden spinner until video playback starts.
- **Sequential Paragraphs**: Revealed one by one as Lord Shiva speaking: *"My child..."*.
- **Navigation Button**: Minimal right arrow (`→`) at the bottom with dark navy background, golden border, and soft glowing hover effect.

### 3. Page 3 – "IT'S MY PROMISE TO YOU"
- **Sacred Visual**: Lord Shiva's divine arm held by the devotee with Rudraksha beads and Tripundra (`assets/images/mahadev_hand_hold.png`).
- **Aspect Ratio Preserved**: Complete image displayed without cropping.
- **Heading**: **"IT'S MY PROMISE TO YOU"** in majestic golden typography with no extra subtitles.
- **Emotional Message**:
  - *"My child,"*
  - *"Whenever you feel weak, remember that you don't have to face everything alone."*
  - *"When your path becomes difficult, I will hold your hand. When you lose your way, I will guide you. When your heart feels heavy, I will give you peace."*
  - *"You may not always see me, but never doubt that my blessings are always with you."*
  - **"Keep walking, my child. I am always by your side."**
  - **"Om Namah Shivaya. 🔱"**
- **Golden Typography**: Highlight on *"I will hold your hand"* with radiant metallic gradient, and soft glowing callout box on the climax.
- **Navigation**: Minimal left arrow (`←`) button returning smoothly to Page 2.

### 4. Background Audio
- Features your uploaded **Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra**.
- Plays continuously across all 3 pages without restarting or stuttering.
- Full volume and play/pause controls in the top-right pill.

### 5. Mobile Responsiveness & Navigation
- All 3 pages scale without cropping on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Touch targets $\ge 44\text{px}$.
- Tap the **`‹ Trishul`** or **`‹ Page 2`** button on the left edge to navigate backward anytime.

## 🚀 How to Update the Website in the Future

Whenever you make changes locally:

1. Open a terminal or PowerShell in this project folder.
2. Stage and commit your changes:
   ```bash
   git add .
   git commit -m "Update birthday experience"
   ```
3. Push to GitHub:
   ```bash
   git push origin main
   ```
4. GitHub Pages will automatically deploy your updates in ~1-2 minutes!
